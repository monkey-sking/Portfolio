作品横跨 UE5 游戏 AI、macOS/iOS 产品与研发工具，底色始终是：懂逻辑、重体验、能交付。 I'm a game AI designer and an independently shipping AI product builder: I've built enemy and combat AI with behavior trees and state machines for a 3A TPS, and I also took a macOS utility end-to-end — requirements, client, backend, and paid distribution — on my own.
In the AI era my value isn't "I can code"; it's turning uncertain AI capabilities into definable, testable, collaboratable systems: knowing what problem to solve, where the boundaries are, and how to land it with programmers and artists.
My work spans UE5 game AI, macOS/iOS products, and developer tooling, with a constant thread: clear logic, great UX, real delivery.
